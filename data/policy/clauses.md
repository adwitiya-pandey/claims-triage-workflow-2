## POL-PX0001
kind: procedure
procedure_code: PX0001
---
Primary care physician office consultation covers initial and follow-up medical examinations, preventive health assessments, and standard chronic condition monitoring. The service must be performed by a licensed general practitioner, family physician, or internal medicine specialist. Coverage applies to diagnostic evaluation and treatment recommendations during standard outpatient clinical visits.

## POL-PX0002
kind: procedure
procedure_code: PX0002
---
Routine diagnostic blood chemistry and metabolic panel testing includes glucose, electrolyte balance, kidney function parameters, and liver enzymes ordered by an attending physician. Specimens must be collected and analyzed by an accredited diagnostic laboratory. Coverage requires clinical indication documented in the patient medical record.

## POL-PX0003
kind: procedure
procedure_code: PX0003
---
Chest radiography examination provides planar diagnostic imaging of the thoracic cavity, including cardiac silhouette and pulmonary fields. Coverage includes standard two-view anterior-posterior and lateral projections ordered to evaluate respiratory symptoms, trauma, or suspected pulmonary pathology. Interpretation must be performed by a credentialed radiologist.

## POL-PX0004
kind: procedure
procedure_code: PX0004
---
Inpatient surgical arthroscopy and joint reconstruction covers surgical intervention for structural joint disorders, ligament reconstruction, and meniscus repair. The procedure requires formal preauthorisation prior to scheduled hospital admission. Operative reports and formal surgical summaries must accompany the reimbursement request.

## POL-PX0005
kind: procedure
procedure_code: PX0005
---
Outpatient physical therapy and rehabilitative exercise sessions are covered to restore physical mobility, muscular strength, and functional capability following acute trauma or orthopedic surgery. Services require a valid physician referral and an individualized therapeutic treatment plan with measurable functional milestones.

## POL-PX0006
kind: procedure
procedure_code: PX0006
---
Diagnostic abdominal ultrasonography covers non-invasive acoustic imaging of abdominal organs, including liver, gallbladder, pancreas, kidneys, and spleen. The examination must be ordered by a physician based on presenting abdominal symptoms or abnormal laboratory findings. Comprehensive sonographic images and a physician interpretation are required.

## POL-PX0007
kind: procedure
procedure_code: PX0007
---
Magnetic resonance imaging of the brain or spine with and without intravenous contrast requires prior clinical preauthorisation and a physician referral. Coverage supports high-resolution diagnostic evaluation of central nervous system pathology, disc herniation, or persistent neurological deficit when conservative management has been documented.

## POL-PX0008
kind: procedure
procedure_code: PX0008
---
Preventive screening and diagnostic colonoscopy provides endoscopic evaluation of the lower gastrointestinal tract. Covered indications include age-appropriate colorectal cancer screening, diagnostic assessment of gastrointestinal bleeding, or surveillance following prior polyp resection. Histopathology findings must be submitted if tissue biopsies are performed.

## POL-PX0009
kind: procedure
procedure_code: PX0009
---
Comprehensive ophthalmologic examination and retinal diagnostic imaging includes visual acuity testing, intraocular pressure measurement, dilated fundus inspection, and optical coherence tomography. Coverage applies when performed to evaluate acute visual impairment, diabetic retinopathy, or established ocular disease.

## POL-PX0010
kind: procedure
procedure_code: PX0010
---
Cardiac catheterization and invasive diagnostic angiography covers hemodynamic assessment, coronary lumen visualization, and ventricular function evaluation in a licensed cardiac catheterization laboratory. The procedure requires clinical preauthorisation, documented pre-procedure non-invasive testing, and a complete post-procedure angiography narrative.

## POL-PX0011
kind: procedure
procedure_code: PX0011
---
Total knee replacement and joint arthroplasty surgery involves prosthetic articular resurfacing for end-stage degenerative osteoarthrosis. Coverage requires clinical preauthorisation, radiographic evidence of severe joint destruction, and documented failure of non-surgical conservative modalities over a minimum three-month period.

## POL-PX0012
kind: procedure
procedure_code: PX0012
---
Targeted biologic immunotherapy and genetic infusion therapies involve specialized biological response modifiers administered under strict clinical supervision. Coverage is restricted to approved medical conditions where standard pharmacological treatments have proven ineffective and formal prior authorization has verified specific plan eligibility.

## POL-PREAUTH-01
kind: coverage
procedure_code:
---
Prior clinical preauthorisation is mandatory for all elective inpatient hospital admissions, advanced radiological examinations, and specialized outpatient surgical interventions. Preauthorisation requests must be submitted at least five business days before the scheduled procedure and include attending physician clinical justification notes.

## POL-PREAUTH-02
kind: process
procedure_code:
---
In the event of an acute emergency admission or unscheduled urgent medical procedure, the preauthorisation requirement is waived provided the treating healthcare facility or member notifies the plan administrator within forty-eight hours of admission or as soon as medically practicable.

## POL-NETWORK-01
kind: coverage
procedure_code:
---
Covered healthcare services provided by participating in-network healthcare facilities and clinicians are reimbursed at the contracted preferred provider allowance, subject to the member applicable plan deductible, copayment percentage, and out-of-pocket maximum thresholds.

## POL-NETWORK-02
kind: coverage
procedure_code:
---
Healthcare services rendered by out-of-network clinicians or non-participating medical facilities are subject to standard review and increased member cost sharing. Out-of-network claims are reimbursed according to the usual, customary, and reasonable fee schedule for the geographic service area.

## POL-DUPLICATE-01
kind: process
procedure_code:
---
When multiple claim submissions reflect identical member identification, healthcare provider, clinical procedure code, and date of service, secondary submissions are treated as potential duplicates. If an earlier claim has been approved or paid, subsequent duplicate claims are flagged for escalation.

## POL-TIMELY-01
kind: process
procedure_code:
---
All healthcare claims must be submitted within ninety calendar days from the date healthcare services were rendered. Claims received after ninety days are considered untimely submissions and require standard supervisory review unless extenuating circumstances are formally documented.

## POL-DOCS-01
kind: documentation
procedure_code:
---
Every claim submission requires a completed standardized claim form accompanied by an itemized hospital or clinician billing statement detailing diagnostic codes, itemized service dates, and billed unit charges. Claims missing mandatory billing documentation are pended for clarification.

## POL-DOCS-02
kind: documentation
procedure_code:
---
Surgical procedures, specialty consultations, and inpatient hospitalizations require corroborating clinical documentation, including formal physician referrals, operative reports, diagnostic imaging interpretations, or discharge summaries supporting the medical necessity of the performed intervention.

## POL-LIMITS-01
kind: coverage
procedure_code:
---
Reimbursement for all covered medical services is subject to the annual benefit limit specified in the member insurance plan contract. When cumulative payments in a plan year exceed the remaining annual limit, subsequent claim amounts are referred for standard adjudication review.

## POL-LIMITS-02
kind: coverage
procedure_code:
---
Procedure-specific maximum allowable amounts establish the upper financial ceiling payable for designated clinical codes. When a billed charge exceeds the plan maximum allowable fee, the excess balance is adjudicated according to network provider contractual agreements.

## POL-EXCL-EXPERIMENTAL
kind: exclusion
procedure_code:
---
Investigational, experimental, or unproven medical treatments, clinical trials, medications, and surgical techniques lacking established consensus safety and efficacy validation from recognized medical regulatory bodies are expressly excluded from plan benefit coverage.

## POL-EXCL-COSMETIC
kind: exclusion
procedure_code:
---
Procedures, medications, and clinical interventions performed primarily for cosmetic, aesthetic, or personal appearance enhancement purposes, rather than the restoration of physiological function or correction of congenital deformity, are excluded from coverage.

## POL-EXCL-OUT-PERIOD
kind: exclusion
procedure_code:
---
Medical treatments and diagnostic services rendered outside the active member policy enrollment period, prior to the effective coverage date, or subsequent to plan termination are excluded from benefit reimbursement under the insurance plan.

## POL-EXCL-INVESTIGATIONAL
kind: exclusion
procedure_code:
---
Diagnostic tests, genetic panels, and therapeutic interventions that are considered investigational or exploratory by the plan clinical review committee are excluded from coverage unless specifically pre-approved under an authorized compassionate care protocol.

## POL-APPEALS-01
kind: process
procedure_code:
---
A member or treating healthcare provider may file an administrative appeal regarding an adverse triage or reimbursement determination within sixty calendar days of receiving notice. Appeals must state the grounds for reconsideration and provide supplemental clinical documentation.

## POL-APPEALS-02
kind: process
procedure_code:
---
Urgent medical appeals concerning ongoing treatment or acute hospital care are reviewed by a medical director within seventy-two hours of submission. Routine appeals undergo administrative and clinical review within thirty calendar days of receipt.

## POL-STATEMENT-01
kind: documentation
procedure_code:
---
Claimant written statements, explanatory letters, and patient correspondence submitted alongside claims documentation are reviewed as supplemental context. Factual claim details, diagnosis records, and provider billing data supersede subjective claimant descriptions.

## POL-SUBROGATION-01
kind: process
procedure_code:
---
When healthcare treatment results from a third-party liability incident or motor vehicle accident, the plan maintains subrogation and reimbursement rights. The claimant must cooperate with claims coordinators and disclose third-party settlement negotiations.
